About the System

VoteSecure is a college/mini-club online voting platform designed around three roles: voter, candidate and administrator.

Core goals

  • Clear election workflow
  • One-vote-per-election enforcement
  • Role-based dashboards
  • Aggregate result publication
  • Notifications, feedback and complaints
  • Auditability and responsive accessibility
